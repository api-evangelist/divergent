# Divergent

> Divergent Technologies, Inc. builds the Divergent Adaptive Production System (DAPS),
> an end-to-end software-hardware production system for industrial digital manufacturing.
> DAPS combines AI-driven generative design software, in-house metal additive manufacturing
> (the Monolith One printer), and automated fixtureless robotic assembly to produce complex
> multi-part metal structures without fixed tooling, for automotive, aerospace and defense
> customers. Founded 2013 in Torrance, California.

## Status for agents

Divergent publishes **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no API reference, no
OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK, no MCP server and no A2A agent card. Divergent's
software (generative design, DAPS orchestration) is delivered as part of a manufacturing
engagement, not as a self-serve developer product. Commercial engagement is through the
contact form or a sales conversation.

- **DAPS (Divergent Adaptive Production System)**: end-to-end digital production system —
  generative design, additive manufacturing, automated fixtureless assembly.
- **Monolith One**: in-house metal additive manufacturing system; aluminum, nickel, steel and
  titanium alloys. Stated by Divergent as not commercially available for purchase or license.
